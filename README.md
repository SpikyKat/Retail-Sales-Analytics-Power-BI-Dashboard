# Retail Business Intelligence & Customer Analytics Dashboard

## Overview

This project delivers an end-to-end retail analytics solution using **Python, SQL, and Power BI** to transform raw retail transaction data into actionable business insights. The project focuses on analyzing sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, product trends, and regional performance through interactive dashboards and data-driven reporting.

## Project Objectives

* Analyze retail sales performance across products, categories, and regions.
* Identify top-performing and underperforming products.
* Monitor key business KPIs such as sales, profit, and order volume.
* Understand customer purchasing behavior and segmentation.
* Provide interactive visualizations for business decision-making.
